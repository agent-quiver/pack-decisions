# Changelog

## 1.1.1 (2026-11-17)

- Discovery queries phrased as the situations people describe, so search finds these skills without their names:
  `ideate` 2.1.2 ("should we use one tool or another for this"), `prd` 1.1.4 ("plan the work for a new feature or
  component") and `adr` 2.1.3 ("record why we picked one option over another"). Nothing an agent reads changes.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-15)

- An icon, a banner and an accent colour for people browsing packs (`media`, pack standard 1.1); agents
  are served nothing new. The pack now follows standard `>=1.1.0`.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

- First open release of `agent-quiver/pack-decisions`.
