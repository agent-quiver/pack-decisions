# Changelog

## 1.1.3 (2026-11-18)

- `adr` 2.1.4, `ideate` 2.1.4, `ticket` 2.1.3 and `standards.docs` 1.2.1: imported from the engineering pack they
  came from, now with `provenance`, so the copies match it again and search shows each once (Quiver 0.25.5).
  `ticket` gains its frontmatter fix: one key per line. `prd` stays 1.1.4, adapted for this pack.

## 1.1.2 (2026-11-17)

- `ideate` 2.1.3: the discovery queries "decide between options" and "compare alternatives before choosing", the
  task types the routing procedure searches with (agent-quiver/pack-routing), in place of two less situational ones.

## 1.1.1 (2026-11-17)

- Discovery queries phrased as the situations people describe, so search finds these skills without their names:
  `ideate` 2.1.2 ("should we use one tool or another for this"), `prd` 1.1.4 ("plan the work for a new feature or
  component") and `adr` 2.1.3 ("record why we picked one option over another"). Nothing an agent reads changes.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-15)

- An icon, a banner and an accent colour for people browsing packs (`media`, pack standard 1.1); agents
  are served nothing new. The pack now follows standard `>=1.1.0`.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

- First open release of `agent-quiver/pack-decisions`.
